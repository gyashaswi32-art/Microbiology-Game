📚 BTY323 MICROBIOLOGY STUDY CODEX

Unit I: Introduction to Microbiology & Cellular Organization

  • Antonie van Leeuwenhoek: First observed living microbes ("animalcules").
  • Louis Pasteur: Disproved spontaneous generation via swan-neck flasks; established Germ Theory.
  • Robert Koch: Formulated Koch's Postulates linking specific pathogens to specific diseases.
  • Prokaryotes vs Eukaryotes: Prokaryotes lack membrane-bound nuclei; contain 70S ribosomes (50S + 30S). Eukaryotes have 80S ribosomes (60S + 40S).
  • Peptidoglycan: Alternating N-acetylglucosamine (NAG) and N-acetylmuramic acid (NAM) cross-linked by peptide chains.

Unit II: Diversity of Microbial World & Growth Kinetics

  • Three Domains (Woese): Bacteria (ester-linked lipids), Archaea (ether-linked branched lipids), Eukarya.
  • Viruses & Phages: Acellular; Capsid + Nucleic acid. Follow lytic (lysis) or lysogenic (prophage) cycles.
  • Media: Selective (e.g. MacConkey agar inhibits Gram-positive), Differential (e.g. Blood agar hemolysis).
  • Growth Curve: Lag Phase (adaptation) -> Log/Exponential Phase ($N_t = N_0 \cdot 2^n$) -> Stationary Phase -> Death Phase.

Unit III: Microbial Metabolism

  • Aerobic Respiration: Complete glucose oxidation via Glycolysis, TCA, and Electron Transport Chain (ETC). $O_2$ is terminal electron acceptor, generating ~32-38 ATP via rotary $F_0F_1$ ATP Synthase.
  • Entner-Doudoroff (ED) Pathway: Unique to Pseudomonas, Rhizobium. Key intermediate: 2-keto-3-deoxy-6-phosphogluconate (KDPG). Net yield: 1 ATP, 1 NADPH, 1 NADH per glucose!
  • Fermentation: Re-oxidizes NADH back to $NAD^+$ in absence of $O_2$ (Lactate / Ethanol reduction).
